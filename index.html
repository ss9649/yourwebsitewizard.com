The studio

Small by design.

I opened Lumen & Rose in 2016 with one chair, a secondhand mirror, and a stubborn belief that a haircut should never feel like an assembly line.

Ten years later the studio is still intentionally small. One guest at a time, no double-booking, no rushing you to the sink. We start every appointment with a real conversation about your hair, your routine, and how much time you actually want to spend on it in the morning.

What you leave with should still look good on day nine — not just under the salon lights.
